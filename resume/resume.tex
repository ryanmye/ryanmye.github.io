  \item Implemented the check's randomization tests (p- and e-values, 11 claim types) and false discovery rate rules; built the Apptainer sandbox and SLURM runs for the agents; stress tests showed it can fail if held-out results leak
\end{itemize}
\subentry{Multi-lab video behavior recognition (MABe)}{Feb 2026 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Beat the frozen-backbone baseline on 9 of 15 lab datasets (13 of 15 on at least one split) by LoRA fine-tuning a video foundation model with a self-authored kinematic auxiliary loss; on one lab, video alone beat the pose-tracking ceiling
  \item Wrote and ran the 8-stage SLURM chain that took all 15 labs from raw video to evaluated 5-fold ensembles, staging up to 1.9 TB of features per lab on node-local NVMe; ran on Blackwell GPUs
  \item Switched the VideoPRISM backbone from JAX to a third-party PyTorch port and checked parity, which enabled LoRA fine-tuning; wrote the PyTorch training stage, video loader, and 21 unit tests
\end{itemize}
\subentry{Calf behavior monitoring (BURE Fellow, CIDA grant)}{May 2025 -- Present}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Built a two-stage posture pipeline (YOLO11n detection, frozen DINO features) on 3,776 hand-labeled calf annotations from 14 pens and 6 cameras: F1 0.93 with 300 labels, 0.98 with 2,988; 0.93+ on unseen cameras, calves, and days
  \item Rebuilt the evaluation (time-blocked test set, McNemar tests with Holm correction, block bootstrap), overturning two earlier claims; self-supervised features beat supervised ImageNet ones by 0.11--0.12 F1 at 50--100 labels
  \item Benchmarked five vision-language models (Qwen, Gemma 3, Gemini 2.5 Pro, PaliGemma, NVLM) for ear-tag and pose recognition; built a Jupyter workflow for veterinary researchers; first-author manuscript in preparation
\end{itemize}
